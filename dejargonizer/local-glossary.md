# Your personal glossary

This file is yours, not the project's. Nothing in it ever leaves this machine, and nothing here is ever sent to Dejargonizer/code-dejargonizer.

Your coding agent adds an entry here when you say "add this to my glossary" - or when it offers you the choice for a term the shipped GLOSSARY.md doesn't cover yet, and you pick "just for me" instead of "flag it for the project." From then on, your agent checks this file before GLOSSARY.md when explaining terms, so your own wording wins for you.

Same shape as every entry in GLOSSARY.md: what the term means in one plain sentence, something to picture, and what to actually do when your agent says it. Delete the example below once you have real entries of your own.
