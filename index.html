The missing translation layer

You don't need to be an engineer. You need a translator.

AI coding agents are astonishing — and they talk like engineers, because they learned from engineers. So you get sentences like "I'll squash-merge the PR once CI is green, then verify the deploy."

Every word of that means something specific and simple. Nobody ever told you what. This is where they tell you.

Engineer says

squash-merge the PR once CI is green

→

Which means

once the automatic checks pass, make the change official.

Three questions you can always ask

Keep these in your back pocket. They work with any agent, any time — and they put you back in control of the conversation.

01

"Say that again with no jargon."

02

"What's about to happen, in one sentence?"

03
