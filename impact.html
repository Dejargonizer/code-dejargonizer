How that coverage number is made

Not by opinion. By a script you can run yourself: node scripts/build-metrics.mjs

  • Agent status lines in the sample—
  • Words marked as jargon, by hand—
  • Of those, explained by a dictionary entry—
  • Matched on the exact term or a listed alias—
  • Lines where every marked word is explained—
  • Lines carrying a decision or an unchecked claim—

The sample lives in data/corpus.json. Each line is a real thing an agent said; the jargon in it is marked by hand as the words a capable person with no coding background would trip on. The script resolves each marked word against GLOSSARY.md and counts. Every figure on this page is regenerated automatically whenever the dictionary or the sample changes.

Words we still cannot explain

The honest half. When the sample contains a word with no entry, it lands here and becomes the next thing to write.

What only you can measure

We collect nothing about your work, so we cannot tell you what changed for you. You can, in about a minute.

At the end of a real session, ask your agent this:

Give me a scorecard for this session: how many technical terms you put in plain English, how many decisions you made for me, how many claims you verified versus assumed, what checks you skipped, and anything you guessed.

That is rule 13 of the skill. Run it once before you install anything and once after, on comparable work. The number that tends to move first is decisions named — because they were always being made, they just were not being said out loud.
